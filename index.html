Customer Guide: Quotation Request

Browse products, create your request PDF, and send it to DUNCO.

Start here

  1. Choose your business sector from the menu at the top.
  2. Tap Products or Browse, search for items, and add the products you need.
  3. Review your basket and quantities, then enter the required business and contact details.
  4. Review the request and tap Download / Save Quotation Request PDF.
  5. Save the PDF on your device and send it to your DUNCO sales representative through WhatsApp or email.

🔎 Find the right product

  • Search by product name, brand, SKU/model, or product type. You do not need to know the exact product name.
  • Examples: Canon, 054, A4 paper, NCR, SSD, or a model/SKU.
  • Use the category filter to narrow results. On a phone, tap Filter to show the filters.
  • If no results appear, try a shorter keyword, a different spelling, the brand, or the model number. Use Reset to clear filters.

🧺 Build your request

  • Tap Add to Request on a product to place it in your basket. The basket count shows how many product lines you selected.
  • Open Quote or Basket to review your selections. Use +/− or the quantity field to change quantities.
  • Add an optional remark when the specification matters, such as colour, pack size, or a preferred model.
  • You can return to browse and add more products before continuing to your details.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari recommended)

  1. Open this catalogue in Safari. If the link was opened inside WhatsApp, use its menu to open it in Safari.
  2. Finish your quotation request and tap Download / Save Quotation Request PDF.
  3. In the iOS print screen, look at the document preview. If needed, spread two fingers outward on the preview to open the PDF preview.
  4. Tap the Share icon.
  5. Choose Save to Files, select a folder such as On My iPhone or iCloud Drive, then tap Save.
  6. Open the Files app to find the PDF. Share it to WhatsApp or attach it to an email.

Tip: The print and share controls can look different between iOS versions. If the print screen does not appear inside an in-app browser, reopen the catalogue in Safari.

🤖 Android

  1. Open the catalogue in Chrome or another full browser.
  2. Finish the request and tap Download / Save Quotation Request PDF.
  3. In the print options, choose Save as PDF as the printer/destination.
  4. Tap the PDF/save button, choose a folder if prompted, and save the file.
  5. Open the saved PDF from Downloads or Files, then share it to WhatsApp or email.

💻 Windows / Linux computer

  1. Open the catalogue in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or another desktop browser.
  2. Finish the request and tap Download / Save Quotation Request PDF.
  3. In the print dialog, select Save as PDF or the equivalent PDF destination.
  4. Click Save and choose where to store the PDF.
  5. Attach the saved PDF to a WhatsApp message or email to your DUNCO sales representative.

Important notes

  • This document is a quotation request, not an official quotation, tax invoice, e-Invoice, or confirmed order.
  • Generating the PDF does not send the request to DUNCO automatically. You must send the saved PDF yourself.
  • Product prices and availability are subject to confirmation in DUNCO's official quotation.
  • Enter your business details carefully before generating the document.

📝 Before you create the PDF

  • Check the item names, model/SKU, quantities, and any remarks in your basket.
  • Enter your business name, contact person, phone number, address, and TIN where requested. If you do not have a TIN or are unsure what to enter, check with your business/accounting team rather than guessing.
  • Review the preview before saving. Keep the PDF so you can attach it to your message to DUNCO.
  • This is a request for a quotation. It does not confirm stock, final pricing, delivery, or an order.
Need help? Contact your DUNCO sales representative and share the saved quotation request PDF.
